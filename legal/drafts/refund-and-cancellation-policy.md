# Refund and Cancellation Policy — DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW

**DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE OR READY FOR PUBLICATION.** Qualified counsel must reconcile this draft with consumer law, the payment provider, actual booking and payout implementation, and each service jurisdiction before launch.

## Before confirming a booking

Show the full agreed price, included services, separate grocery/travel expenses, payment schedule, cancellation windows, refund eligibility, and any non-refundable charge before the customer accepts. Do not charge or characterize a deposit as refundable/non-refundable unless the implemented payment and lawful policy support that term.

## Customer cancellation

Proposed policy parameters to be completed by counsel and the operator:

- Cancellation more than [X] hours before service: [REFUND/FEES].
- Cancellation within [Y] hours: [REFUND/FEES].
- Provider has already purchased groceries: reconcile itemized receipts, returnable items, and approved expenses under [RULE].
- Recurring services: cancellation and pause rules for each future session [RULE].

## Provider cancellation or inability to accommodate

If the provider declines a disclosed allergy request or cannot lawfully provide the selected service, stop the booking and apply the approved refund process. Do not pressure a provider to accept a risk. If the provider cancels for another reason, notify the customer and offer the remedy permitted by applicable law and the accepted terms.

## Changed quotes and grocery reconciliation

A revised price requires clear notice and affirmative customer acceptance before it becomes part of the agreement or is charged. Grocery expenses must be itemized and supported by a receipt available to the customer. Handle amounts above an approved grocery budget under a clear approval process; do not charge unexplained overages.

## Payment disputes and timing

Publish only payment/refund timing confirmed by the payment provider. Keep card handling with the payment provider; never store raw card data. Explain how to contact support and the payment provider, and how disputes or suspected safety incidents are handled.
