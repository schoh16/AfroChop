## 1. Marketplace role

AfroChop provides a marketplace through which customers may discover independent cooks and chefs, communicate about services, request quotes, and manage bookings. The legal relationship between AfroChop, customers, and providers, and the obligations of each party, must be defined by counsel for each service and jurisdiction. Do not describe providers as employees or AfroChop as a food seller unless counsel confirms that treatment.

## 2. Eligibility and account information

Users must provide accurate information, protect account credentials, and use the service lawfully. Customer contact, location, dietary, and allergy details must be handled only for permitted marketplace and safety purposes and in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

## 3. Service requests, quotes, and changes

A request is not a confirmed booking merely because it was submitted or a quote was received. A booking becomes confirmed only after the customer accepts the final terms, required provider responses are recorded, and the payment and confirmation steps configured for that service are completed. Any provider-initiated price change must be clearly itemized, communicated, and accepted by the customer before it changes the agreed total. ## 4. Food allergies and dietary needs

Customers must disclose known allergies and dietary restrictions before confirming a booking and review the current food-allergy notice. Providers must review disclosed allergies and explicitly accept or decline whether they can accommodate the request. A request with disclosed allergies must not proceed to booking confirmation or payment capture until that response is recorded.

Ingredient lists and allergen disclosures are based on information available to the provider and may not capture every ingredient or process risk. Substitutions, shared equipment, supplier changes, and cross-contact can create serious risks. AfroChop must not represent a dish as guaranteed allergen-free without a verified, substantiated basis. Customers should contact the provider through AfroChop before relying on any food-safety information. The operational controls in the Food Allergy and Dietary Safety Policy apply; this section does not replace them.

## 5. Food safety and provider qualifications

Providers must comply with applicable food-safety, licensing, permit, and consumer-protection rules. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, food, preparation location, and service type. AfroChop may require documentation, limit services, suspend accounts, or request additional review. A displayed verification state is not a guarantee of safety or legal compliance.

## 6. Payments, cancellations, refunds, and disputes

Applicable prices, grocery expenses, travel fees, platform fees, deposits, balances, taxes, cancellation terms, and refund rules must be shown before a customer accepts a booking. The payment provider and marketplace payment flow must be described accurately; AfroChop must not state that funds were charged, protected, or refunded unless the payment system confirms it. ## 7. Safety incidents

Customers should promptly report suspected illness, allergen exposure, unsafe handling, or another urgent food-safety concern using the in-app urgent reporting route and contact emergency services where needed. Incident reports may be escalated, investigated, and shared with appropriate parties or authorities when legally required or necessary to address a safety threat.

## 8. Privacy, records, and account closure

Sensitive allergy and dietary information is restricted to people who need it to perform or administer the requested service. Records are retained according to the approved retention schedule and applicable legal holds. The final Privacy Policy describes access, correction, deletion, and other rights that depend on location.

Any warranties, limitations, indemnities, dispute-resolution terms, choice of law, and mandatory consumer rights must be drafted and approved by qualified counsel. Nothing in this draft represents that AfroChop is immune from liability or may disclaim non-waivable legal duties.
