# Food Safety Incident Reporting Procedure — DRAFT FOR OPERATIONAL AND COUNSEL REVIEW

**DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE OR READY FOR OPERATION.** Before launch, name a staffed safety lead and backups, validate emergency routing, and have counsel/qualified food-safety professionals approve the procedure.

## Intake

Accept reports of suspected allergen exposure, illness, contamination, unsafe handling/storage, labeling errors, permit concerns, and recalls. Record reporter, affected booking/provider if known, what happened, when it occurred, severity, contact preference, and any evidence voluntarily supplied. Avoid requesting unnecessary medical details.

## Urgent reports

Mark a report urgent at intake when a person may be in immediate danger or a serious exposure/illness is alleged. Timestamp and escalate it immediately to the designated on-call safety contact. Tell the reporter to contact emergency services or a qualified medical professional if someone may need urgent care; support staff must not diagnose or prescribe treatment. The in-app urgent route is not an emergency service.

## Preserve and triage

Restrict case access to authorized safety/admin personnel. Preserve relevant booking request, customer acknowledgment and notice version, allergy disclosure, provider response, menu and ingredient/allergen versions, messages, quote/payment references, grocery receipts, permits, and incident actions. Apply a documented legal hold where appropriate. Record every access and action.

## Investigation and containment

Assess whether to pause an affected listing/service, suspend a provider, notify potentially affected customers, contact suppliers, preserve samples/labels/receipts, or notify a regulator or insurer. Make decisions based on qualified review and applicable law; do not wait for a complete investigation before taking proportionate immediate safety precautions.

## Resolution

Record investigation findings, communications, remedies, referrals, regulator notifications, corrective actions, and rationale. Reinstatement after a serious incident requires authorized, documented review. Keep incident records under the counsel-approved retention schedule, with legal holds.
