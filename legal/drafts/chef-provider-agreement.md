## 1. Independent provider and marketplace

The provider offers the services expressly listed in the approved profile. The parties' legal status, tax treatment, insurance responsibilities, and allocation of duties must be confirmed by counsel; this draft does not decide employment or agency status.

## 2. Qualifications and permits

The provider must supply truthful identity, experience, food-handling, business-license, permit, insurance, and kitchen information requested for the provider's jurisdiction and service type. The provider must keep documents current, disclose expiration or suspension, and stop offering a service when a required authorization is absent, expired, or suspended. AfroChop may verify, restrict, or suspend services pending review. Verification is not a safety guarantee.

## 3. Menu, ingredient, and allergen disclosures

For every menu item, the provider must identify known ingredients, the applicable major allergens, and known cross-contact risks. The provider must update disclosures when recipes, suppliers, equipment, preparation locations, or processes change. The provider must not claim “allergen-free” or equivalent certainty unless the claim is substantiated and approved under AfroChop's applicable controls.

## 4. Allergy-request response

The provider must review each customer's disclosed allergens and dietary needs before quoting or confirming the service. The provider must explicitly accept that the request can be accommodated or decline it. A booking request with a disclosed allergy remains blocked from proceeding until that response is recorded. A decline stops the request; the provider must not proceed with preparation.

## 5. Food safety and incident cooperation

The provider must follow applicable law and AfroChop's approved food-safety standards, use safe sourcing, preparation, storage, transport, and sanitation, and promptly report suspected exposure, illness, unsafe conditions, or recall issues. The provider must preserve relevant receipts, communications, recipe and ingredient records, and cooperate with a reasonable investigation and lawful authority requests.

## 6. Quotes, expenses, and customer communications

The provider must itemize service charges and grocery, travel, or other approved expenses. A revised quote or price is not effective until the customer accepts it through AfroChop. Grocery receipts must be submitted through the designated workflow and must accurately reflect purchases. Providers must communicate through permitted channels and protect customer addresses and sensitive dietary information.

## 7. Records and data protection

The provider may use customer allergy, dietary, address, and booking data only as necessary to provide the requested service and meet legal duties. The provider must not retain or disclose such data beyond the approved retention schedule, except for lawful recordkeeping or a documented legal hold. AfroChop may investigate reports, pause listings, restrict service types, request new documentation, or suspend an account where safety or compliance concerns require it.
