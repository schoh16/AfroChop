# AfroChop legal and safety drafts

**DRAFTS — NOT APPROVED FOR USE.** These templates are starting points, not legal advice. Qualified counsel familiar with each operating jurisdiction must review and adapt them before publication, accepting users, or launching paid food services. Food-safety professionals and privacy counsel should review the operational standards and data practices as well.

The placeholders, operating rules, statutory disclosures, deadlines, insurance requirements, and retention periods must be completed by AfroChop and counsel. Do not publish a draft merely by removing this notice.

Drafts included: [Customer Terms of Service](./customer-terms-of-service.md), [Chef Provider Agreement](./chef-provider-agreement.md), [Food Allergy and Dietary Safety Policy](./food-allergy-and-dietary-safety-policy.md), [Food Safety Standards](./food-safety-standards.md), [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md), [Refund and Cancellation Policy](./refund-and-cancellation-policy.md), [Incident Reporting Procedure](./incident-reporting-procedure.md), and [Records Retention Policy](./records-retention-policy.md).
